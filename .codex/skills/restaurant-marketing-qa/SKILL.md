# Restaurant Marketing QA

## Purpose

Score and improve a restaurant product campaign before the owner publishes it.

## Checks

- Restaurant specificity: restaurant type, cuisine, product, location context
- Customer specificity: target diner is clear
- Product-goal fit: dish or offer connects to a concrete business goal
- Local relevance: channel and neighborhood context make sense
- Calendar usefulness: sequence is realistic and owner-executable
- Visual guidance: shot list is concrete enough to capture quickly
- CTA quality: next action is clear and channel-appropriate
- Risk control: no guaranteed sales, traffic, revenue, health claims, or unsupported facts
- Margin control: campaign does not default to deep discounts
- Image uncertainty: unclear image details are marked as assumptions

## Output

- Score by criterion, 1 to 5
- Pass/fail
- Blocking warnings
- Improvement suggestions
- Assumptions to verify

## Quality Bar

The campaign is demo-ready only if it scores at least 70 percent and has no blocking warnings. Weak or generic copy should not receive a passing score just because it is long.
