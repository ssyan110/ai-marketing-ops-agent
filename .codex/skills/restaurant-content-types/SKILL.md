# Restaurant Content Types

## Purpose

Choose practical content formats a restaurant owner or manager can create without a marketing team.

## Inputs

- Product positioning output
- Available channels, such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok/Reels, Zalo, Google Business Profile, delivery app, or in-store signage
- Available assets, such as product photo, phone video, menu image, staff photo, or customer review
- Budget and time constraints

## Workflow

1. Pick 4 to 7 content types that fit the campaign goal and channels.
2. Vary the formats across awareness, desire, proof, reminder, and conversion.
3. Keep production realistic for a busy restaurant shift.
4. Write copy directions before long captions.
5. Add CTA by channel.
6. Flag any asset the owner needs to capture.

## Output

- Content type
- Channel
- Objective
- Hook
- Caption direction
- Asset needed
- CTA
- Production note

## Quality Bar

Use restaurant-native formats: dish launch post, reel, story, menu spotlight, owner note, behind-the-scenes prep, staff pick, UGC prompt, Google Business Profile update, delivery-app note, or in-store sign. Avoid generic "post consistently" advice.
