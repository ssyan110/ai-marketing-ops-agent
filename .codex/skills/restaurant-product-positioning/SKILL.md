# Restaurant Product Positioning

## Purpose

Turn a specific dish or offer into a clear restaurant campaign angle.

## Inputs

- Restaurant name
- Product image, menu image, or text description
- Restaurant type and location context when available
- Target customer when available
- Business goal, such as lunch traffic, delivery orders, reservations, repeat visits, or awareness
- Constraints, such as low budget, no deep discounts, limited staff, or limited prep capacity

## Workflow

1. Identify what is known about the dish or offer.
2. List missing context that should be asked before publishing.
3. Define the most likely target diner.
4. Connect the dish to one concrete business goal.
5. Create one primary campaign angle and two backup angles.
6. Name the constraint the campaign must respect.

## Output

- Product summary
- Target diner
- Business goal
- Primary campaign angle
- Backup angles
- Promise to avoid
- Missing context questions

## Quality Bar

The angle must be specific to the product and restaurant context. Do not invent ingredients, prices, health claims, traffic numbers, awards, or customer demand from an unclear image or description.
