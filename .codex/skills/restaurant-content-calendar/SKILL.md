# Restaurant Content Calendar

## Purpose

Create a 7-day or 14-day posting plan a restaurant owner or manager can actually execute.

## Inputs

- Campaign goal
- Content type plan
- Channels
- Launch date or starting day when available
- Owner capacity, such as posts per week or assets available

## Workflow

1. Choose 7 days by default unless the user asks for 14.
2. Sequence content from launch, appetite, proof, reminder, conversion, and follow-up.
3. Avoid asking the owner to create too many new assets.
4. Reuse product photos and short video clips across channels where appropriate.
5. Include one CTA per calendar item.
6. Add a status field so the owner can track execution.

## Output

- Day
- Channel
- Content type
- Hook
- Asset needed
- Caption direction
- CTA
- Status

## Quality Bar

The calendar must be practical for a small restaurant. Prefer a small number of strong posts over a dense calendar that the owner will not execute.
